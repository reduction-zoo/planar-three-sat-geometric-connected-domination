# Drawing construction and bounds

This candidate argument reconstructs Tamassia–Tollis, [*A Unified Approach to Visibility Representations of Planar Graphs* (1986), Algorithm W-VISIBILITY, p. 328, Theorem 1, p. 330](https://workshop.tcs.uj.edu.pl/mszana2015/gutkraw/unified.pdf), also presented in [Duncan–Goodrich, §7.2.3](https://cs.brown.edu/people/rtamassi/gdhandbook/chapters/orthogonal.pdf), and replaces each bar by one of 46 explicitly checked constant templates. It requires a biconnected planar graph of maximum degree four with at least three vertices. The reduction's core premise is proved in `proof.md`. No independent review has yet accepted this argument.

## Ordering and visibility

Choose edge st. Removing it leaves an s–t path because the graph is biconnected. Start with that path's vertex order. For each component C outside the current order, biconnectivity implies at least two distinct neighbors a,b in the ordered set. Choose the first two in order, find an a–b path with internal vertices in C, and insert those internal vertices immediately after a. Existing relative orders remain unchanged. The inserted vertices each have an earlier and a later path neighbor; old vertices retain theirs. At most V insertions cover the graph. Thus s is the only source, t the only sink, and every edge is oriented from smaller to larger order. All searches and insertions are polynomial and deterministic.

Since st is an edge, choose its left face as exterior so the rest of the embedding lies on its right. The plane st-graph visibility theorem supplies consecutive incoming/outgoing blocks and the acyclic left-to-right dual with st's dual arc omitted. `visibility.py` enumerates the embedding's faces and constructs this dual. Distinct topological face ranks satisfy every required left-to-right inequality. Place each non-st edge vertically at the rank of its left face, between the st-order ranks of its endpoints. Give each vertex the horizontal span of its incident columns. These are the standard visibility coordinates, tightened to the incident-column span, which cannot create an intersection. Place st in column −1, outside all other bars, extending only the s and t bars to it. Only intended edge/bar incidences occur. There are O(V) face ranks and vertex ranks, so the bar coordinates have magnitude O(V). To match the primary algorithm exactly, take auxiliary face potentials A(f)=2 rank(f) and edge columns J(e)=2 rank(left(e))+1; every dual arc has A(right)−A(left)≥2, so A(left)<J(e)<A(right). Use J(st)=−1. Applying the global affine map x=(J−1)/2 gives precisely the implemented columns, including x(st)=−1. The primary proof needs these inequalities, not minimum possible potentials. Its optional extension of a zero-length degree-two bar is unnecessary here: tightening back to the common incidence point preserves nonintersection, and the catalog explicitly includes that top/bottom pattern.

## Point replacement

At each bar list its distinct incident columns from left to right. Each column has an incoming edge below, an outgoing edge above, or both. Same-side coincidences cannot occur in a visibility representation. With degree two to four there are exactly 46 patterns. The complete catalog is `rounds/032/templates.json`; the independent `test_templates.py` checks its completeness through Boolean top/bottom subset enumeration and checks all geometric paths. This finite catalog is part of the algorithm, not a runtime solver call.

For k columns a template lies in [0,2k] × [−4,4]. Terminals are at (2i+1,±4), and all other path vertices have |y|≤3. Its paths are simple and pairwise disjoint except for their common center. Multiply all bar coordinates by 20. Map odd template x-values to actual incident columns, internal even values to the midpoint between their adjacent columns, and the two outside values to four units outside the extreme columns. This gives a strictly increasing integer map, extended piecewise linearly. Translate template y-values by the scaled bar height. Monotone horizontal stretching preserves all path intersections and compass directions.

Distinct bars have height separation at least 20, so their strips have a gap of at least 12. A nonincident vertical segment has an integer column outside the unscaled bar interval; after scaling it is at least 20 units from that interval. The local routing extends by only four. Outside all strips, join the matched top/bottom terminals along the original vertical segment. Hence routes do not cross, overlap, or pass through unrelated vertices. Each original vertex is one point, each edge is a simple orthogonal path, and incident paths share only the original point. Removing collinear points preserves these properties.

## Bounds and compact attempt

The catalog is constant size, so the expanded graph has O(V) bends. Its coordinates have magnitude O(V), its total integer-grid route length is O(V²), and its coordinate bit lengths are O(log V). The elementary ear-order implementation is polynomial (a loose O(V³) bound suffices). The exact layout validator also runs in polynomial time by enumerating the O(V²) route lattice points and suppressing bend vertices.

`checked_layout` first permits tsmpy only on at most 1,000 core vertices, under a fixed allowance of 50,000,000 Python line events. This is a deterministic finite computation bound, not a wall-clock deadline or an oracle cutoff. The bounded input size and fixed implementation also bound native work and integer bit lengths per event; the library's fixed constants are harmless on this bounded optional branch. Any exception or allowance exhaustion discards the attempt. Before acceptance, require at most 100V drawn vertices, integer coordinates of magnitude at most 100V, and exact topology/nonintersection validation. Thus an accepted compact drawing has O(V²) route length even without trusting its method. Every rejection, and every larger core, uses the explicit visibility construction. The branch changes output coordinates, not the proof of the graph interface.

The complete F construction scales the accepted integer drawing by ten before adding its paths and side points. This preserves O(V²) target size and O(log V) coordinate bit length. F and G repeat the same deterministic drawing procedure; the fixed operation allowance is not chosen from machine timing.
